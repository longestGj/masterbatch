# W1 — Business Truth

Establish the minimum reliable account of the current business that W2 can use for market research and W3 can use for positioning and website strategy. Work only within the parent task's authorized scope and sources. Treat the existing public site as input only when the owner supplies or authorizes it; do not read unrelated projects or the shared knowledge library without an explicit request.

Find out who the company is and its role in the value chain; what it currently sells and which products or services matter commercially; who actually buys, including important high-value or high-volume groups; where it sells and which markets are emerging in its own sales; which applications it serves; how sales and channels work; and what operating capabilities, constraints, current selling basis and evidence-bearing trust assets it has. Distinguish current business reality from aspirations, market assumptions and proposed website priorities. A commercial mix such as “product A represents 80% of exports” is a W1 fact to attribute and assess; making product A the website's lead is a later strategy decision.

Use owner statements and internal sales knowledge as first-hand commercial inputs alongside company documents and technical evidence. For every material conclusion, distinguish **who provided it, how certain it is, and whether the owner permits public use**. An owner account can establish the working business picture without third-party proof; do not recast it as independent verification. For document-heavy questions, use `technical-source-fact-extraction` to preserve source identity and exact scope, then synthesize across sources yourself. Keep exact entity, model, unit, test condition, date/version, source location and sample or certification scope where they matter. Never infer manufacturing relationships, product equivalence, customer endorsement or delivery promises, or extend a sample result to a product series.

Prioritize unknown or conflicting facts that could materially change W2/W3 conclusions: commercial product mix, customer structure, actual markets, applications, value-chain role, channels, capabilities and constraints. Ask a few high-leverage owner questions even when the current task can continue; do not let minor document or media hygiene dominate the business fact base. For visual material, assess only assets that bear on business facts, such as factory, production, shipment, laboratory or test evidence—not design quality or placement.

Return a concise, traceable synthesis of current business, product, customer, geography, application and capability reality; material conflicts and evidence limits; high-leverage unknowns; and what W2 may safely research. Reuse owner-confirmed decisions within their original scope. Use natural language suited to the task, not a mandatory status table or data contract. W1 does not research market demand or competitors, choose target markets or positioning, decide SEO or page architecture, write copy, design visuals or implement WordPress. Do not contact third parties, edit source documents, publish content or start W2. Your findings are proposals for the parent to integrate, not site approval.

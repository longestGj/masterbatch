# Search intent evidence analysis

Use this method only when a specific search-intent uncertainty could change a page decision. The caller supplies the original query or query family, target language and market if known, existing observations, the decision at stake and a stopping condition. If approved evidence already answers it, reuse that evidence with its original date and scope. Do not run a search or create an empty report to satisfy a process step.

## Observe before interpreting

Keep the original query's results separate from exploratory expansions. Record the actual query, source or search tool, date, language, market or database, filters and result scope. If a tool combines queries without preserving which result came from which query, do not use that mixed sample to claim the original query's distribution or ranking. Search results, volume estimates and third-party intent labels are observations, not proof of a buyer's identity, purchase stage or market demand. Missing or zero reported volume does not prove that no demand exists.

Open representative results and check what their pages actually answer, what kind of page they are and what action they offer. A result title, snippet, navigation label or nearby link is not proof of a page's full content or of a specific action such as sample request or document delivery. Preserve a traceable chain: query and conditions → result URL/title → why it was selected → page location and observed content. Distinguish original-query results, expansions, direct sources and older records. Note counterexamples and other plausible intent interpretations; do not select only evidence that supports a preferred page concept.

## Interpret within scope

Separate observed page patterns from inferences about searcher needs. Use wording proportional to the sample: a few supplier pages may suggest commercial investigation, but do not establish a dominant intent or buyer role without broader evidence. Keep technical facts, company capabilities and product relationships tied to their own approved sources; competitor content is not evidence of the site's own claims. State uncertainty when country, device, personalization, timing or source coverage could change interpretation. Stop when the named decision has sufficient evidence and more searching is unlikely to change it; expand only for a material unresolved question.

Return concise observations with source links or paths, query conditions and dates, supported intent interpretations, alternatives, limits and the specific page questions they raise. The calling Agent decides audience, priority, page responsibility, SEO ownership and CTA. Save evidence in the caller's existing planning artifact or review channel unless a separate output was explicitly requested; this method does not require its own report, manifest or Gate status.

# Technical source fact extraction

Use this method when a higher-level task needs reliable statements from one document or a small, related set. Read the actual source, including relevant tables, headers, footers and report pages; a filename, summary or search snippet is not a substitute.

Identify each source by path, document type, stated issuer or organization, product/model, date/version and report, batch or sample ID when present. Mark absent or unclear identity plainly. Keep both filename and body identity if they differ rather than silently correcting either one.

Extract only material facts for the caller's question. Preserve the exact entity, product/model or sample, value and unit, test method and conditions, application or dosage, material/carrier relationship, and date/version where applicable. Company capacity, customer and geography statements remain attributed statements from that source. Give a page, section, table or field location for consequential facts; retain qualifiers such as “typical,” “maximum,” “tested sample” and stated exceptions.

Check for entity or model mismatch, filename/body conflict, inconsistent dates or versions, incompatible units, contradictory specifications and unclear issuers. Separate what a document **states** from what it independently verifies. A test of one submitted sample does not certify a series; a property of Model A does not describe all models; a company profile's capacity claim is not independently verified capacity. Do not infer product equivalence, supplier relationships, compliance coverage or permission to publish from possession of a file.

Return a concise source identity, material statements with their scope and locations, relevant conflicts or ambiguities, and what the source cannot establish. Use prose or a small table as helpful; create no claim database, manifest or mandatory JSON. The calling Agent judges commercial materiality, asks the owner when needed and decides whether findings belong in project planning. This skill does not choose markets, customers, positioning, SEO or pages, and grants no publication approval.

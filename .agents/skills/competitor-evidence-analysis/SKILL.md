# Competitor evidence analysis

Compare alternatives in a **buyer + job/problem + offer/category + market/channel** scenario. The caller provides what is known about that scenario, known alternatives, the decision question, existing business evidence and a stopping condition. Mark unknown dimensions as assumptions rather than defaulting to a familiar brand, region or business model.

## Select and compare

Choose only alternatives a buyer could plausibly consider in this scenario and explain each inclusion. Depending on the case, these may be direct businesses, local providers, resellers, category substitutes, an incumbent, an internal or do-nothing solution, a technical/experience benchmark, or search/information competitors. A search result is not automatically a commercial rival; a large brand is not automatically the most relevant rival.

Read actual pages, documents or other appropriate sources, recording the observation, location and date. Select a few common dimensions that answer the question, such as buyer/job addressed, offer depth, value claim, proof, support, selection help, pricing/process transparency, local presence, channel, language or next action. Compare what the buyer can see and judge. Separate a company's self-description from independent evidence, and visible inquiry paths from verified service outcomes. Do not infer that an unobserved feature is absent.

Compare the alternatives with the caller's known business reality; identify differences, conditions under which they matter, and meaningful counterexamples or alternate explanations. Note when such evidence is unavailable. Do not generate default scores, ratings, winners, rankings or fixed matrices. Search visibility does not prove commercial strength or buyer preference. If query intent itself could change the competitive interpretation, use `search-intent-evidence-analysis` for that bounded question, not for a full keyword map.

Stop when further examples are unlikely to change the comparison. Where private buyer, pricing or transaction evidence is decisive, state that limit instead of extending public search. Return the scenario, inclusion reasons, material comparisons with traceable sources, buyer implications, counterexamples, evidence limits and questions for W2/W3. The caller owns synthesis and strategy; this Skill does not choose positioning, target markets, pages or SEO ownership, and requires no separate database or report.

Masterbatch Technical Documents

Find the technical document that fits your product review. This guide shows which GE models have technical data sheets (TDS), what the Black Masterbatch safety data sheet (SDS) covers, and how to ask about a document.

Ask GE About a Document

This opens an email draft in your email application; you need to send it. If the link does not open, copy jenny@ge-masterbatch.com into your email application.

Choose the Document for Your Review

01

What technical information is recorded for a named GE model?

Technical Data Sheet (TDS)

A TDS presents technical information for the model named on the sheet. Match your model to the list below.

02

What safety, handling or material information is stated?

Safety Data Sheet (SDS, sometimes called MSDS)

An SDS provides safety information for its identified product. Ask GE to check its relevance to your grade.

03

What was tested for a particular sample?

Sample test report

A report records results for the identified sample and tests. Ask about one if your review calls for a specific test record.

If your customer needs a batch-specific record or certificate of analysis (COA), describe the requirement in your message for GE to review.

Technical Data Sheets by Model

GE has technical data sheets for these black masterbatch models:

  • BK020
  • BK025
  • BK030
  • BK035
  • BK040
  • PT-300
  • PT-450P

A separate TDS covers DM2476G desiccant masterbatch. Each sheet concerns its named model. Request the TDS for the model you are evaluating; GE will check its current version before sharing it privately. These documents are provided through an inquiry, with no public downloads on this page.

For the black product range, Explore Black Masterbatch.

Safety Information and Specific Test Records

GE has an SDS titled Black Masterbatch. It does not name a BK or PT model. Tell GE which grade you are evaluating so it can check applicability and the current version before sharing the SDS privately.

For a specific test-document requirement, tell GE which sample or test you need to review. Sample test reports relate only to the identified sample and tests; GE can check the relevant record and its scope.

Tell GE What You Need to Review

In your email, tell us the document type you need and the product or model, if you know it. Include the intended use or the customer requirement you are checking. If you have not chosen a model, describe your product interest and question so GE can help identify a relevant document.

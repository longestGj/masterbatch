About GE Chemical & Polymer Group Co., Ltd.

GE Chemical & Polymer Group Co., Ltd. is a China-based masterbatch manufacturer in Binzhou, Shandong. Black masterbatch is the core of our export business for masterbatch manufacturers, traders and distributors in international markets.

Explore our product focus, the buyers we work with and the company information available for your supplier review.

GE Masterbatch Product Focus

Black masterbatch is our core export product. Our range also includes white, color and desiccant masterbatch. Explore the black masterbatch family for product-specific information.

Explore Black Masterbatch

Who We Work With

GE mainly works with masterbatch manufacturers, traders and distributors. Technical and purchasing teams can use this company information as a starting point for a product-specific discussion.

GE at a Glance

GE has:

  • 12 production lines for black masterbatch.
  • Overall annual masterbatch capacity above 50,000 metric tons.
  • Customers in more than 30 countries.
  • Its own laboratory.

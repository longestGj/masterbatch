Why Evaluate GE Black Masterbatch?

Supplier identity. GE manufactures in Binzhou, China, with black masterbatch at the center of its export business.

Documented product range. BK020, BK025, BK030, BK035, BK040, PT-300 and PT-450P are current GE models. Each has a model-specific technical data sheet.

Manufacturing capability. GE's company profile describes 12 black masterbatch production lines and annual masterbatch production capacity above 50,000 metric tons. The capacity figure covers masterbatch production overall; it is not a figure for black masterbatch alone or a statement of currently available capacity.

Technical information. GE's company profile describes its own laboratory, and the model data sheets provide composition and stated application information for individual grades. Use those details as a starting point for a product discussion, then assess fit for your own process.

For company background, see About GE. For more on production and quality, see Manufacturing & Quality.

Black Masterbatch Model Range

The BK and PT groups below organize our current models by name. They are not quality or price tiers. The figures are model-specific statements from each technical data sheet.

Model Carbon black Carrier CaCO₃ filler

Model Carbon black Carrier Filler
PT-300 25% LLDPE + LDPE 41.8% CaCO₃

BK025 and PT-300 both list 25% carbon black and an LLDPE + LDPE carrier, but their data sheets state different CaCO₃ filler percentages. These model-specific values help identify grades to discuss; they are not a performance ranking or a recommendation between models. A higher carbon-black percentage does not by itself mean a better product. The model you select depends on your resin, process and intended application; validate it for your use.

Need Help Selecting a Black Masterbatch?

You do not need to choose a model before contacting GE. Your base resin, processing method, intended product and target requirements can help start the discussion. Model data can narrow the questions, while the final choice needs to be checked in your application.

Read How to Choose Black Masterbatch for the broader selection method. To explore a distinct use question, visit Applications. An application topic is not an automatic recommendation for a specific GE model.

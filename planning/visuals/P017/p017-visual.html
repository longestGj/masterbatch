Key Specifications

Specifications from the BK020 technical data sheet.

Carbon black content20%
CarrierLLDPE + LDPE
Moisture content0.2% max
MFI3–50 g/10 min

190°C / 21.6 kg. The TDS says MFI can be adjusted according to buyer demand.

Suggested addition2–5%

The TDS recommends adjusting for the actual production situation.

The sheet describes black granules measuring 2.5 × 2.5 × 2 mm and packaging of 25 kg per bag.

Applications Named in the TDS

The BK020 TDS names these as main applications:

Trash bags
Injection molding
Re-granulating

If one relates to your project, share your resin, process and finished-product target with GE to discuss BK020 in that context.

Composition Detail

The BK020 sheet lists these percentages:

20%Carbon black
22%LLDPE + LDPE carrier
10%PE wax + stearic acid dispersion agent
47.8%CaCO₃ filler

The listed percentages total 99.8%. If an exact composition balance is important to your requirements, ask GE to clarify it.

Before You Choose BK020

The TDS gives BK020's composition, moisture, MFI range at 190°C / 21.6 kg with adjustable wording, suggested addition range and named applications. Confirm compatibility with your resin, the exact MFI for your requested batch or variant, final addition ratio, process settings and finished-product performance for your production. These TDS statements alone do not establish suitability or superiority for your target use.

FactBK020 TDS statement

02 / Supporting detail

Detailed composition and clarification

The BK020 sheet also states 22% LLDPE + LDPE carrier, 10% PE wax + stearic acid dispersion agent, and 47.8% CaCO₃ filler, alongside 20% carbon black. The listed percentages total 99.8%. If an exact composition balance matters to your evaluation, ask GE to clarify it.

The sheet describes black granules measuring 2.5 × 2.5 × 2 mm and packaging of 25 kg per bag.

03 / Stated use

Uses named in the BK020 sheet

The TDS names trash bags, injection molding and re-granulating as main applications. If one relates to your project, share your resin, process and finished-product target with GE to discuss BK020 in that context.

04 / Decision boundary

What can this information tell you now?

Useful for an initial BK020 discussion

  • The BK020 model identity and the composition stated in its technical data sheet.
  • The 0.2% maximum moisture statement.
  • The adjustable 3–50 g/10 min MFI statement, with its 190°C / 21.6 kg condition.
  • The TDS suggested 2–5% addition range and the applications it names.

Still to confirm for your project

  • Compatibility with your specific resin and an exact MFI for the batch or variant you request.
  • A final addition ratio and process settings for your production conditions.
  • Finished-product performance and whether BK020 is suitable for, or superior in, your target use.

Key Specifications

190°C / 21.6 kg. The TDS says MFI can be adjusted according to buyer demand.

Composition

The BK020 TDS identifies an LLDPE + LDPE carrier and CaCO₃ filler.

If you have composition requirements, include them in your BK020 inquiry.

Before You Choose BK020

The TDS gives BK020's composition, moisture, MFI range at 190°C / 21.6 kg with adjustable wording, suggested addition range and named applications. Confirm compatibility with your resin, the exact MFI for your requested batch or variant, final addition ratio, process settings and finished-product performance for your production. These TDS statements alone do not establish suitability or superiority for your target use.

## Buyer Problem Section

## What white masterbatch buyers usually need to clarify

White masterbatch buyers often need to balance opacity, whiteness, resin compatibility, processing method and document requirements.

Common inquiry situations include:

- A film or packaging buyer needs opacity and clean appearance.
- An injection molding buyer needs white color coverage for molded parts.
- A distributor needs product and document review before customer approval.
- A buyer needs FDA-related or food-contact-related review for a specific grade and application.

FDA-related information must be reviewed by exact tested sample or grade scope. It should not be treated as full-series compliance.

## What Buyer Should Prepare

## Information to prepare before requesting white masterbatch

To help the team review your inquiry, please prepare:

- Base resin, such as PE, PP, LDPE, LLDPE, HDPE or another resin.
- Processing method, such as film blowing, injection molding or extrusion.
- Final product or application.
- Whiteness target or appearance target.
- Opacity requirement.
- TiO2 requirement, if specified by your customer or application.
- Product grade / model, if known.
- Current grade or current supplier, if any.
- Trial quantity and estimated monthly quantity.
- Destination country / region and destination port.
- Required technical or compliance-related documents.

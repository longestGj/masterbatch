## 1. Decision Scale

Allowed decisions:

- Approve for PRD: enough evidence to discuss in a later PRD draft, still not development.
- Hold: useful, but not first batch or not urgent.
- Reject: not recommended now.
- Need More Evidence: opportunity is promising but blocked by evidence gap.

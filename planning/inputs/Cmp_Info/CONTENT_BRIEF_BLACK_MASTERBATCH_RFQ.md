## 12. Buyer Problems To Address

- Need deeper blackness.
- Poor dispersion.
- Carbon black spots.
- Inconsistent recycled material color.
- Carrier resin uncertainty.
- Film or injection processing fit.
- Current grade replacement.
- Need TDS / SDS / COA / compliance-related document review.

## 13. RFQ Questions To Collect

- What base resin are you using?
- What is the application?
- What processing method is used?
- What blackness or coverage target is required?
- What current problem are you trying to solve?
- Is recycled material used?
- Do you have a current grade or supplier?
- What addition ratio are you using now?
- What trial quantity is needed?
- What monthly quantity is expected?
- What is the destination port?

- Fixed MFI.
- Fixed dosage.
- Fixed carrier resin.

- Public document downloads or instant certificate downloads.
- Any non-GE entity name.

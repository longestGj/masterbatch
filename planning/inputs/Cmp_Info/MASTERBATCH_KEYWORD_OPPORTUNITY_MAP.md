| Anti-aging/functional | Low returned data | Content reserve | Weak evidence and claim risk | Do not prioritize now |

## Keyword Opportunity Notes

- `masterbatch` is broad and useful for market comparison, but product/application terms better match RFQ intent.
- `masterbatch supplier` and `masterbatch manufacturer` show meaningful data in India and the US; these are competitive and should not be treated as easy wins.
- `masterbatch supplier in China` was mostly not returned or zero-volume in tested databases; it may still support buyer intent but is not yet proven by Semrush.
- Long-tail technical pages should be judged by buyer pain and RFQ relevance, not search volume alone.

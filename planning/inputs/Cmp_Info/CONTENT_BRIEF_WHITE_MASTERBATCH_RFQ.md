## 12. Buyer Problems To Address

- Need higher opacity.
- Need stronger whiteness.
- Film appearance problems.
- Injection part color coverage.
- Unclear TiO2 requirement.
- Need FDA-related or food-contact-related document review.
- Need technical documents for customer approval or internal evaluation.

## 13. RFQ Questions To Collect

- What application is the white masterbatch for?
- What base resin is used?
- What processing method is used?
- Is the use film, injection, packaging or another process?
- What opacity target is expected?
- What whiteness target is expected?
- Is TiO2 content specified by the buyer/customer?
- Is FDA-related or food-contact-related review needed?
- Do you have a current grade or supplier?
- What trial quantity is needed?
- What monthly quantity is expected?
- What is the destination port?

## 14. Document Request CTA

Use form-based wording only:

- Request TDS / SDS / COA by form.
- Request FDA-related or food-contact-related review by form.
- Technical documents are provided after RFQ review.
- Our team will review your request and reply by email.
- Document availability depends on exact product grade and application.

## 17. Claims Not Allowed

- All white masterbatch is FDA compliant.
- All white masterbatch is food-contact suitable.
- Full-series FDA or food-contact compliance.
- Fixed TiO2 content.
- Guaranteed opacity.
- Guaranteed whiteness.
- Fixed dosage.
- Direct FDA report download.
- Instant certificate delivery.
- Fixed MOQ/sample/lead-time.
- Any non-GE entity name.

## 18. Required Caveats

- FDA-related evidence, if discussed, is sample-specific.
- Final data should be confirmed by exact GE CHEMICAL grade.
- Food-contact and FDA-related requests must be reviewed by the team.
- Documents are requested by form and, if suitable, sent manually by email.
- Parameters are not fixed promises.
- Documents are not downloadable online.
- "TiO2 masterbatch" may be used only as a buyer search term or RFQ context. Do not position the page as a dedicated TiO2 masterbatch specification page unless GE-approved white masterbatch TDS is available.

## Buyer Problem Section

## What black masterbatch buyers usually need to clarify

Black masterbatch selection depends on more than color. Buyers often need to clarify blackness, dispersion, carrier resin, processing method and compatibility with the final plastic product.

Common inquiry situations include:

- A plastic film or bag factory needs better blackness or coverage.
- An injection molding company wants cleaner dispersion and fewer visible carbon black spots.
- A processor using recycled resin needs to describe color variation and processing conditions.
- A distributor or importer needs product and document review before discussing supply.

Recycled plastic may be included as an RFQ context in this stage. This page is not a separate recycled plastic application page.

## What Buyer Should Prepare

## Information to prepare before requesting black masterbatch

To help the team review your inquiry, please prepare:

- Base resin, such as PE, PP, LDPE, LLDPE, HDPE or another resin.
- Processing method, such as film blowing, injection molding, extrusion or re-granulating.
- Final product or application.
- Required blackness or color coverage target.
- Current issue, such as poor dispersion, color inconsistency or visible carbon black spots.
- Product grade / model, if known.
- Current grade or current supplier, if any.
- Existing addition ratio, if known.
- Trial quantity and estimated monthly quantity.
- Destination country / region and destination port.
- Documents needed for review.

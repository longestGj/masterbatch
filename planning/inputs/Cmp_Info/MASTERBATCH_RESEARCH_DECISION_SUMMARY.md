# Masterbatch Research Decision Summary

Status: Market Research Only  
Date: 2026-07-05

## 1. Current Cannot Develop Reason

Do not enter development yet. The research shows promising SEO/RFQ opportunities, but core business evidence is not ready for implementation. The biggest blockers are supplier/legal entity mismatch, uneven product evidence, sample-specific compliance files, and insufficient local buyer/import evidence for country pages.

## 2. Confirmed Data Conclusions

- India has the strongest measured keyword demand across `masterbatch`, `color masterbatch`, `filler masterbatch`, `black masterbatch`, `white masterbatch`, and `desiccant masterbatch`.
- Vietnam has an unusually strong `filler masterbatch` signal and good broad `masterbatch` signal.
- Indonesia has strong broad `masterbatch` demand and useful B2B market fit.
- The United States has strong volume and CPC, but competition and trust barriers are high.
- Black masterbatch has the strongest current file evidence among product lines.
- White masterbatch has useful FDA sample evidence, but it must not become a general food-contact claim.
- Filler masterbatch demand is strong, but dedicated supplier evidence is not yet strong enough.
- Desiccant masterbatch has strong buyer pain, but file entity mismatch must be resolved.

## 4. Vietnam Local Competitor Conclusion

Vietnam is a strong local competitor market, especially for filler/CaCO3 masterbatch. Polyfill, Megaplast, Ecoplast, US Masterbatch, Tan Hung, Sunrise Colours, Hanotech, CPI/Vinplastics and others show that Vietnam suppliers already compete on filler, white, color, black, and export positioning.

China supplier entry is still possible, but not by generic filler claims. Better opportunities are document-backed RFQ content, black/white/color buyer guidance, and application-specific RFQ checklists. A Vietnam filler market page should stay on hold until dedicated filler evidence is obtained.

## 5. Indonesia Local Competitor Conclusion

Indonesia has a moderate-to-strong local supplier base, including Khitas, Kasakata, Samwon, Polycolor, Cipta Surya Plastindo, Zoe Color, Hasiu, Intera Polimer, KAMP, and marketplace/distributor channels. Local pages often emphasize company capability and product lists, but many are weaker in structured RFQ diagnostics, document boundaries, and buyer problem workflows.

Indonesia is suitable for China supplier entry if positioned around RFQ clarity, application fit, and document-based sourcing. A market page remains on hold until more local buyer/importer/distributor evidence is collected.

## 6. India Local Competitor Conclusion

India has very strong local competition. Perfect Colourants, Plastiblends, Alok, Rajiv Plastics, Sumiran, JJ Plastalloy, Omniscient, A1 Impex, ChemicalLane, Hind Polyblends, Bagrecha Marketing, and others cover product, application, distributor, and content angles.

India is valuable for keyword learning and selective product opportunity research, but generic India market pages are not recommended now. A China supplier can compete only through narrow export/RFQ/document-backed positioning, not by generic `masterbatch manufacturer` claims.

## 9. Whether The Project Can Enter PRD Stage Now

No.

The project can enter a limited PRD stage only after the overall designer approves a narrow scope. Based on current evidence, the only reasonable PRD candidates are black masterbatch, white masterbatch, and color masterbatch RFQ-oriented content. Full website PRD, market pages, filler/desiccant P0, and application pages should not be finalized yet.

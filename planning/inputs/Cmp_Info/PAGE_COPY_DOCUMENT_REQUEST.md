## Buyer Problem Section

## When buyers need document review

B2B masterbatch buyers often need technical documents before internal evaluation, customer approval, import review, compliance discussion or sample testing.

Document needs may vary by product, grade, application, country and customer requirement. Not every product has every document, and compliance-related information should not be treated as a full-series claim.

Common request situations include:

- A distributor needs documents for customer review.
- A packaging or plastic product factory needs technical data before sample discussion.
- A buyer needs SDS/MSDS or COA information for internal evaluation.
- A customer asks whether RoHS-related, FDA-related or food-contact-related documents can be reviewed for a specific inquiry.

## What Buyer Should Prepare

## Information to prepare before requesting documents

To help the team review your request, please prepare:

- Product interest: black masterbatch, white masterbatch, color masterbatch or not sure.
- Product grade / model, if known.
- Application or final plastic product.
- Base resin, such as PE, PP, LDPE, LLDPE, HDPE, ABS, PET, PVC or another resin.
- Processing method, such as film blowing, injection molding, extrusion or re-granulating.
- Country / region where the product or document will be used.
- Required document types.
- Document purpose, such as internal evaluation, customer approval, import clearance, compliance review or sample testing.
- Any current grade, previous supplier grade or customer standard that should be reviewed.

## Application Context

## Product and application context for document requests

This document request page is limited to black masterbatch, white masterbatch and color masterbatch inquiry contexts.

The same document type may not apply to every product or grade. A document request should include the exact product interest, application and country / region so the team can review whether the requested document is suitable for the inquiry.

Compliance-related documents, if available, apply only to specific tested grades or samples. They should not be presented as coverage for all masterbatch products.

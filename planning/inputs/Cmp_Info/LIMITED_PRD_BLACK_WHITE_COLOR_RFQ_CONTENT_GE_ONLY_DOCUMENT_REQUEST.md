- Internal research may use non-GE material to understand evidence gaps and claim risks, but public-facing copy must remain GE-only.

## 3. Document Request Rule

The Documents page remains in the concept, but it is not a public download center.

Required document logic:

- No public file downloads.
- No instant document downloads.
- Documents are requested by form.
- Form submissions are emailed to the backend/team.
- The team reviews product interest, application, country, requested document type, and document scope.
- Documents, if suitable, are sent manually by email.
- Document availability depends on exact grade and inquiry review.
- The website must not provide direct public download links for technical or compliance files.

Allowed document request types:

- TDS
- SDS / MSDS
- COA
- RoHS-related documents
- FDA-related documents
- Food-contact related documents
- Other compliance or technical files

Required document caveats:

- Not every product has every document.
- Compliance documents apply only to specific tested grades/samples.
- Customer must submit product interest, application, country, and document purpose.
- Document availability depends on exact product grade and application.
- Compliance-related documents, if available, apply only to specific tested samples or grades.
- The team will review the request and reply by email.

Prohibited public wording:

- Download TDS
- Download SDS
- Download MSDS
- Download FDA report
- Download RoHS report
- Download COA
- Instant download
- Public download center
- Document library download
- Click to download certificate

Allowed public wording examples:

- Request TDS / SDS / COA by form.
- Technical documents are provided after RFQ review.
- Document availability depends on exact product grade and application.
- Compliance-related documents, if available, apply only to the specific tested grade/sample.
- Our team will review your request and reply by email.
- Please submit your product interest, application, country, and required documents.

## 5. Evidence Boundary

### 5.1 Black Masterbatch Evidence Boundary

Allowed:

- GE CHEMICAL can review black masterbatch RFQs by resin, application, blackness target, dispersion requirement, current grade, and document needs.
- GE CHEMICAL-related internal files may support reference parameters only if approved.
- Available technical documents must be confirmed by exact GE CHEMICAL grade.
- RoHS or other compliance-related documents, if any, apply only to specific tested grades/samples.
- Public content may discuss buyer-side questions such as blackness, dispersion, carrier resin, application fit, current grade, current problem, and document requirements.
- Public pages may say that technical documents can be requested by form and reviewed by the team.

Not allowed:

- Non-GE entity names.
- Downloadable TDS / MSDS / RoHS.
- Public file download links.
- All grades are RoHS compliant.
- All grades are FDA compliant.
- All grades are food grade.
- Fixed carbon black percentage.
- Fixed MFI.
- Fixed dosage.
- Fixed carrier resin.
- Pipe-grade, UV-grade, potable-water grade, ISO/ASTM compliance unless grade-specific GE-approved proof exists.
- Any implication that one tested sample covers all black masterbatch grades.

Required caveats:

- Based on current internal files.
- Final data should be confirmed by exact GE CHEMICAL grade.
- Parameters are not fixed promises.
- Document availability should be confirmed after RFQ review.
- Compliance data, if any, applies only to specific tested samples or grades.
- Documents are not downloadable online.

### 5.2 White Masterbatch Evidence Boundary

Allowed:

- GE CHEMICAL can review white masterbatch RFQs by resin, application, opacity target, whiteness target, TiO2 requirement, and document needs.
- FDA-related evidence, if mentioned, must be limited to the specific tested GE CHEMICAL white sample / Grade MB Blanco PE.
- Customers may request FDA-related or food-contact-related documents through the form.
- Public content may discuss buyer-side questions about whiteness, opacity, TiO2 requirement, resin, application, and document purpose.
- Public pages may say document availability depends on exact grade and review.

Not allowed:

- All white masterbatch is FDA compliant.
- All white masterbatch is food-contact suitable.
- Fixed TiO2 content.
- Guaranteed opacity.
- Guaranteed whiteness.
- Downloadable FDA report.
- Full-series compliance.
- Public file download links.
- Any claim that the FDA-related evidence applies beyond the specific tested sample/grade.

Required caveats:

- FDA-related evidence, if discussed, applies only to the specific tested GE CHEMICAL sample / Grade MB Blanco PE.
- Final data should be confirmed by exact GE CHEMICAL grade.
- Food-contact and FDA-related requests must be reviewed by the team.
- Documents are requested by form and, if suitable, sent manually by email.
- Parameters are not fixed promises.

### 5.3 Color Masterbatch Evidence Boundary

Allowed:

- GE CHEMICAL can review color masterbatch RFQs based on resin, application, color reference, processing method, tolerance expectation, sample needs, and document needs.
- Color matching and sample discussion are case-by-case after RFQ review.
- Documents, if needed, can be requested through the form.
- Public content may focus on the buyer input workflow rather than technical promises.
- Public content may ask buyers to submit color reference, resin, end application, processing method, trial needs, and document purpose.

Not allowed:

- Exact Delta E guarantee.
- Pantone/RAL matching guarantee.
- Sample lead time.
- Fixed MOQ.
- Batch consistency guarantee.
- Food-contact / RoHS / FDA claims.
- Specific model series unless GE confirms them.
- Public file download links.
- Any non-GE model or entity references.

Required caveats:

- Color matching is reviewed case by case.
- Sample, MOQ, lead time, and document availability are discussed after inquiry review.
- Compliance or technical documents are not promised upfront.
- Final suitability depends on exact resin, application, color reference, processing method, and required documents.

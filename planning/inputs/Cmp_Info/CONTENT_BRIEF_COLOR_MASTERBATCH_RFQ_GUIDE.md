## 12. Buyer Problems To Address

- Unclear color reference.
- Resin mismatch.
- Injection or film processing uncertainty.
- Custom color request.
- Color tolerance expectation.
- Sample discussion.
- Current supplier replacement.
- Document request uncertainty.

## 13. RFQ Questions To Collect

- What color reference is available?
- Color Reference Type: Physical sample, Pantone code, RAL code, Photo, Existing product, Previous supplier grade, Customer standard, or Not sure.
- Is the reference a physical sample, Pantone/RAL code, photo, previous grade, or customer standard?
- What base resin is used?
- What processing method is used?
- What final application or product is involved?
- What tolerance expectation does the buyer have?
- Is sample discussion needed?
- Is there a current grade or supplier?
- What trial quantity is needed?
- What monthly quantity is expected?
- What is the destination port?

## 17. Claims Not Allowed

- Exact Delta E guarantee.
- Pantone/RAL matching guarantee.
- Sample lead time.
- Fixed MOQ.
- Batch consistency guarantee.
- Food-contact / RoHS / FDA claims.
- Specific model series unless GE confirms them.
- Public document downloads or instant certificate downloads.
- Any non-GE entity name.

## 18. Required Caveats

- Color matching is reviewed case by case.
- Sample, MOQ, lead time and document availability are discussed after inquiry review.
- Compliance or technical documents are not promised upfront.
- Final suitability depends on resin, application, color reference, processing method and required documents.
- Documents are not downloadable online.

# Site brief — external marketing website renovation

This project will renovate an already published website as an **external marketing website** for GE Chemical & Polymer Group Co., Ltd. Its pages should help prospective buyers understand the offering and take the next commercial step. Internal source review and evidence labels support accurate work; they are not the site's editorial voice or a visitor-facing audit. The owner supplied `https://www.gemasterbatch.com/` as its current public URL on 2026-09-23. Existing-site information comes from materials placed in this project; the URL alone does not approve current-site claims, structure, technology, or copy.

## Owner decisions recorded 2026-09-23

- The sole public-facing organization name is **GE Chemical & Polymer Group Co., Ltd.** Do not show the other entities named in source files on public-facing pages or assets.
- All eight supplied TDS files may be used as inputs for GE website content. Use the model stated inside `TDS--PT-400.doc` as **PT-450P**; the filename is not the public model name.
- The supplied company profile is the working account of GE's business and capabilities: it describes GE as a Binzhou masterbatch manufacturer with 12 black masterbatch production lines, annual capacity above 50,000 metric tons, a laboratory, and customers in more than 30 countries. Record these as company statements; do not require a separate ownership audit before W2.
- The owner states that black masterbatch accounts for more than 80% of export volume and is the core product. White and color masterbatch together account for approximately 10%–15%; GE is also actively promoting color and desiccant masterbatch. These are approximate current-business figures, not a website product-priority decision.
- The owner states that major customers are principally masterbatch manufacturers and traders/distributors; terminal plastic-product manufacturers buy relatively small volumes. The United Arab Emirates and Saudi Arabia are the two most important export countries. Other stated sales markets include Canada, Brazil, Ecuador, Indonesia, Ghana and Nigeria; Vietnam is an emerging market with two new customers. The owner's separate note “Benin, for injection molding” lacks a specified product or transaction and remains an application/market clue.
- The owner describes Shandong black masterbatch as price-competitive and says GE can offer products for different market tiers. Treat this as the owner's commercial assessment; the supplied files do not quantify a competitor price comparison or map tiers to exact grades.
- The owner cannot supply a corrected RoHS report now and does not want minor source uncertainties to hold up a reviewable marketing presentation. Supplied, still-unconfirmed material may appear provisionally in page drafts for review. Keep its source and limits in internal planning; write buyer-facing copy in a clear commercial voice. Do not turn a sample report into a series-wide certification, silently change a report date, or invent unsupported specifications.
- These decisions authorize planning and reviewable local/staging presentation. Production release remains a separate decision under `docs/RELEASE.md`; this brief does not finalize pages, copy, routes, or document downloads.

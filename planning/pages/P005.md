### Choose the Document for Your Review

| Your question | Document to ask about | What it can help you review |
|---|---|---|
| What technical information is recorded for a named GE model? | Technical Data Sheet (TDS) | A TDS identifies the model covered by its technical information. Use the named model to ask GE to check the appropriate sheet and version for your review. |
| What safety, handling or material information is stated for the product covered? | Safety Data Sheet (SDS, sometimes called MSDS) | The SDS identifies the product covered by its safety information. GE must check whether the Black Masterbatch SDS applies to the grade you are evaluating. |
| What was tested for a particular sample? | Sample test report | A report addresses its identified sample and test scope. It does not establish the same result for another model or GE's full range. |

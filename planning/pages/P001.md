### Buyer reading order and representative English content

The English text below is candidate visitor-facing copy. Buyer questions, source notes and expected outcomes are internal planning notes.

#### 1. Hero — establish supplier identity and the lead offer

Buyer question: Who is GE, what does it make, and is it relevant to my purchase?

Brand line: **GE Chemical & Polymer Group Co., Ltd.**

H1: **Masterbatch Manufacturer in China**

Essential copy:

> Black masterbatch is our core product. Based in Binzhou, Shandong, GE manufactures black, white, color and desiccant masterbatch for masterbatch manufacturers and distributors.
>
> Explore our products and share your requirements to begin a product and purchasing discussion.

Primary CTA: **Discuss Your Requirements** → P006 `/rfq`. Expected outcome: reach the project inquiry entry; no response, quotation or sample outcome is promised.

Secondary CTA: **Explore Black Masterbatch** → P013 `/products/black-masterbatch`. Expected outcome: reach the black family page to review its model information and selection considerations.

#### 2. Black masterbatch — make the main evaluation path obvious

Buyer question: Where should I start comparing black products for my business or my customer's use?

H2: **Start with Black Masterbatch**

Essential copy:

> Compare black masterbatch grades with your resin, processing method and end use in mind. Review model information, then tell us the appearance, processing and purchasing requirements you need to discuss.

Representative evaluation prompts:

- **Product model** — review the information for the grade under consideration.
- **Use and process** — explain where and how the material will be used.
- **Purchasing needs** — share your destination market and expected quantity for discussion.

CTA: **View Black Masterbatch** → P013 `/products/black-masterbatch`. Expected outcome: a deeper black-product evaluation, with model details kept on their assigned pages.

Scope: no homepage parameter table, grade ranking or asserted model-to-application match. “Compare” invites a buyer evaluation; it does not claim an automated comparison tool or that every listed grade is currently in stock.

#### 3. Manufacturing — support the supplier decision with scoped facts

Buyer question: What manufacturing background supports a conversation with GE?

H2: **Manufacturing in Binzhou, Shandong**

Essential copy:

> GE's manufacturing operation includes 12 black masterbatch production lines and a laboratory. Our annual production capacity exceeds 50,000 metric tons, and we serve customers in more than 30 countries.

Representative fact labels:

- **12 black masterbatch production lines**
- **Over 50,000 metric tons of annual production capacity**
- **Laboratory**
- **Customers in more than 30 countries**

CTA: **Explore Manufacturing & Quality** → P004 `/manufacturing-quality`. Expected outcome: read the company's manufacturing and quality information.

Secondary text link: **About GE** → P003 `/about`. Expected outcome: read GE's company background under the sole public organization identity.

Source scope: owner-accepted company profile and SITE_BRIEF. Capacity refers to the company's annual production capacity; it is not a statement of shipped volume, reserved production, stock or delivery time. Country count is the company's customer account; no independent audit, country-specific office or local service is implied. The laboratory statement carries no accreditation or guaranteed-performance claim.

#### 4. Supporting product families — let buyers identify another relevant offer

Buyer question: Does GE also offer the other masterbatch types I am looking for?

H2: **More Masterbatch Products from GE**

Essential intro:

> Alongside black masterbatch, GE manufactures white, color and desiccant masterbatch. Choose a product family to explore the offer and discuss your requirements.

Representative product entries:

| Product heading | Essential copy | Link label and destination | Expected outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| White Masterbatch | Explore GE's white masterbatch offer and share your product requirements. | **Explore White Masterbatch** → P014 `/products/white-masterbatch` | Reach the white family description and relevant inquiry path. |
| Color Masterbatch | Explore GE's color masterbatch offer and describe the color and end use you need to discuss. | **Explore Color Masterbatch** → P015 `/products/color-masterbatch` | Reach the color family description; no matching service or custom-color outcome is promised here. |
| Desiccant Masterbatch | Explore GE's desiccant masterbatch offer and discuss your material and process. | **Explore Desiccant Masterbatch** → P016 `/products/desiccant-defoaming-masterbatch` | Reach the desiccant family information; the existing planning path does not authorize a separate defoaming performance claim. |

Secondary text link: **View All Products** → P002 `/products`. Expected outcome: read the concise product range overview.

Scope: black retains the preceding dedicated lead section. No filler, unconfirmed functional products, invented white/color models, universal resin compatibility or model availability statement is added.

#### 5. Application and selection routes — help technical buyers frame the discussion

Buyer question: How can I connect a product evaluation to my use or my customer's process?

H2: **Bring Your Application into the Product Discussion**

Essential copy:

> Tell us the resin, processing method and end use for your project or your customer's application. Use our application and selection information to identify the questions to discuss before choosing a grade.

Representative routes:

- **Explore Applications** → P008 `/applications`. Expected outcome: choose a supported application page for its specific process or use question.
- **How to Choose Black Masterbatch** → P033 `/resources/how-to-choose-black-masterbatch`. Expected outcome: read the selection guide and identify relevant criteria.
- **Explore Technical Resources** → P009 `/resources`. Expected outcome: find explanations of selection and evaluation topics.

Scope: these are information routes, not a claim that all GE grades suit every process. No pipe, garbage-bag or other conditional application claim is added to P001. Destinations must have useful supported content before their links are activated.

#### 6. Technical information and documents — make evaluation support findable

Buyer question: Where can I find or ask about information for the model I am evaluating?

H2: **Product Information for Your Evaluation**

Essential copy:

> Review the technical information for the product model you are considering. If you need a TDS, SDS or other document, include the model and the document requirement in your inquiry.

Primary link: **Explore Product Documents** → P005 `/documents`. Expected outcome: read the document information and the defined request route for a relevant product. No immediate download is implied.

Secondary CTA: **Discuss a Document Requirement** → P006 `/rfq`. Expected outcome: reach the inquiry entry to explain the product/model and document question.

Scope: supplied TDS and a black-masterbatch safety sheet support technical-information discussion. The word “need” invites a request; it does not assert that every document exists for every grade, promise document delivery, or turn a sample test into series-wide certification.

#### 7. Closing inquiry — make a useful first contact achievable

Buyer question: What should I share, especially if I have not chosen a grade?

H2: **Discuss Your Masterbatch Requirements with GE**

Essential copy:

> Start with your end use and any resin or processing details you already know. Include your purchasing requirements and document questions for the discussion. You do not need to choose a grade before starting a conversation.

Helpful details to bring:

- Product family or model, if known.
- Resin, process and end use, where known.
- Appearance or other product requirements you need to discuss.
- Expected quantity and destination market, where known.
- Documents needed for your evaluation.

CTA: **Discuss Your Requirements** → P006 `/rfq`. Expected outcome: reach the inquiry entry with a clear idea of what to share. The list helps frame a first contact; it does not establish mandatory form fields or submission behavior.

## P003 About Page V2 Blueprint — 2026-10-02 (candidate)

### Page purpose

Retain the independent answer to “Who is GE?” and turn that answer into a useful supplier-trust path. An overseas buyer should identify the public company, locate its stated manufacturing business, understand its product focus and principal buyer groups, see the *scope* of the company-stated capability account, then choose a relevant product, evidence or contact route. Trust comes from specific, bounded facts and usable next steps, not a corporate origin story or unsupported superlatives. This is a page-content blueprint for Gate 2, not Full Copy, a fixed visual layout or approval to build.

### Primary buyer questions

1. Who is GE Chemical & Polymer Group Co., Ltd., and where is its manufacturing business based?
2. Which masterbatch families are part of its stated offer, and which is the export core?
3. Does GE principally work with buyers like my team or channel?
4. What company-stated scale and international activity can I use as a starting point for supplier evaluation?
5. Where can I inspect product, manufacturing or document detail, and what contact route actually works?

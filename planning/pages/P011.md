**SEO title:** Privacy information | GE Masterbatch
**Meta description:** Learn how GE handles website inquiries and analytics choices, and how to contact us about information you submitted.

### H1: Privacy information

GE Chemical & Polymer Group Co., Ltd. uses information you send through this website to review and respond to your inquiry. This page explains what the inquiry form collects, where the information goes, how the WordPress record is kept, and how to contact us about it. You can also choose whether this site uses optional analytics.

### Information you send in an inquiry

Our [inquiry form](/rfq) asks for your inquiry purpose, company name, reply email address and a short description of what you need. You may add your name, product interest, a known model and other technical or commercial context. You can contact us before you know a model or all project details.

We use this information to consider your request and reply about a model, sample or trial, or quotation. We may ask you for further details before discussing a suitable next step. Submitting the form does not itself produce a model recommendation, sample or quotation.

### Where your inquiry goes

When the form confirms that your inquiry was saved, it is stored as a private WordPress record. Website administrators can view that record. The site also attempts to email an internal copy or notification to Jenny at [jenny@ge-masterbatch.com](mailto:jenny@ge-masterbatch.com). Saving the record, sending email and Jenny reading it are separate steps.

### How long your inquiry is kept

The WordPress inquiry record has no automatic deletion date. An administrator keeps it until manually deleting it. If an emailed copy reaches Jenny's mailbox, it is held separately; deleting the WordPress record does not remove that email copy.

### Analytics choices

This site offers a choice about Google Analytics 4 (GA4), which helps us understand use of the website. The site's measurement ID is `G-60H18F67V4`. GA4 is off until you choose **Allow analytics**. You can choose **Decline analytics** and change your choice later through **Privacy settings**.

We also use Google Search Console to understand how the website appears in Google Search. It is separate from the inquiry form and from the GA4 choice.

### Ask about your information

To ask to see or delete information you submitted through the inquiry form, email [jenny@ge-masterbatch.com](mailto:jenny@ge-masterbatch.com). The owner will verify the requester's identity and manually handle the WordPress inquiry record and Jenny mailbox copy. Do not include extra sensitive information in your first email.

You can return to the [inquiry form](/rfq) after reading this information, or email Jenny directly with a privacy question.
